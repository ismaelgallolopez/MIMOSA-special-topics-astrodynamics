% With $\beta$ values from \autoref{tab:wp2_lagrange_shift}, it can be compared against the provided in \cite{ancona2024recent}. 
The highest required $\beta = 0.00810$ (from \autoref{tab:wp2_lagrange_shift}) is lower than already-flown missions, such as GAMA Alpha (0.00923), LightSail 1 and LightSail 2 (0.00971) \parencite{ancona2024recent}. 
% This can also be observed in \autoref{fig:wp2_beta_comparison}, where the required $\beta$ for the L2 shift is plotted against the $\beta$ of past solar sail missions.
For that reason, the mission is deemed feasible with current technology, at least in terms of the required lightness number.
