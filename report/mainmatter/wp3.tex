\subsection{Task 3.1b}

To assess the asymptotic stability of the Lagrange points, we analyze the real part of the eignevalues in \autoref{tab:eigenvalues} \parencite[sec 8.3]{battin1987introduction}.
An eigenvalue with positive real part indicates an unstable libration point \parencite{mcinnes1999artificial}. 
Since $\lambda_4$ lays on the right complex plane, all of the points are unstable. 
When accounting for the solar sail acceleration, the (un)stability behaviour does not change, and the effect on the eigenvalues is minimal. 
This was a foreseeable result, since the demonstrations in the literature \parencite{simmons1985restricted, bombardelli2011stability, aliasi2011artificial} already stated that in the solar-sail augmented \ac{cr3bp} the L2 Lagrange point is always unstable.

\section{Task 3.2}

Verification for the eigenvalues is performed by several different techniques. 
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The classical \ac{cr3bp} is reduced to the trivial case of $\mu = 0$ (see \autoref{sec:trivial-cr3bp}), where the eigenvalues can be derived analytically and agree with the report's and \cite[Appendix I, table A]{szebehely1967theory}
  \footnote{Note that for the specific $\mu = 0 $ case, the comparison shall be performed against the book's L3.}.
  \item The eigenvalues at the equilibrium points for the same case can be found in \cite[Appendix I, table A]{szebehely1967theory}; the results agree 
  \footnote{Note that the reference swaps the definition of L1 and L2 (see \cite[Fig 4.1]{szebehely1967theory}), so the L2 values presented in this report correspond to the book's L1, and vice versa. 
  Also, the results agree up to the 9th decimal place, leaving only some possible difference due to hypothetical rounding errors or the technique performed by the author, from which no details could be found}.
  \item The characteristic polinomial \parencite[sec 3.1.2]{mcinnes2000strategies} is used to compute the eigenvalues for the augmented \ac{cr3bp}, and the results also agree.
  \item For the augmented \ac{cr3bp}, nonlinear \acs{eom} (\cite{mcinnes2000strategies}) are integrated and compared to the linearized system used for computing the eigenvalues (\autoref{fig:eigenvalue_verification}), showing good agreement \parencite[see][section 6.3]{strogatz2024nonlinear}.
\end{enumerate}

Thus, the results are verified.
