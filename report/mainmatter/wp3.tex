\subsection{Task 3.1b}

To assess the asymptotic stability of the Lagrange points, we analyze the real part of the eignevalues in \autoref{tab:eigenvalues} \parencite[sec 8.3]{battin1987introduction}.
An eigenvalue with positive real part indicates an unstable libration point \parencite{mcinnes1999artificial}. 
Since $\lambda_2$ lays on the right complex plane, all of the points are unstable. 
When accounting for the solar sail acceleration, the (un)stability behaviour does not change, and the effect on the eigenvalues is minimal. 
This was a foreseeable result, since the demonstrations in the literature \parencite{simmons1985restricted, bombardelli2011stability, aliasi2011artificial} already stated that in the solar-sail augmented \ac{cr3bp} the L2 Lagrange point is always unstable.

\section{Task 3.2}

Verification for the eigenvalues is performed by several different techniques.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item For the classical \ac{cr3bp}, the eigenvalues at the equilibrium points are derived analytically in the trivial case $\mu = 0$ (see \autoref{sec:trivial-cr3bp}) and, for the assigned $\mu$, compared against the tabulated values of \cite[Appendix I, table A]{szebehely1967theory}; both agree.
  % \footnote{The reference swaps the definitions of $L_1$ and $L_2$ (see \cite[Fig 4.1]{szebehely1967theory}), so the report's $L_2$ values correspond to the book's $L_1$, and vice versa. Results agree to the 9th decimal place.}
  \item For the augmented \ac{cr3bp}, the in-plane eigenvalues at the displaced Sun-Earth $L_2$ reproduce the independently computed values of \cite[Table 2]{farres2019road} for $\beta = 0.03$.
  \footnote{The mass ratio reported in \cite{farres2019road} ($\mu = 3.0034806\times10^{-6}$) reproduces neither their tabulated equilibrium position nor their eigenvalues; the implied value $\mu = 3.0404234\times10^{-6}$ recovers both ($x_{L_2} = 1.00762463476$, $\lambda = 3.72982$, $\omega_1 = 2.8482$) to all reported digits, and is adopted only for this comparison.}
  \item The nonlinear \acs{eom} \parencite{mcinnes2000strategies} are integrated and reproduce the growth predicted by the eigenvalues, themselves derived from the potential Hessian (\autoref{fig:eigenvalue_verification}) \parencite[see][sec 6.3]{strogatz2024nonlinear}.
\end{enumerate}
Thus, the results are verified.
