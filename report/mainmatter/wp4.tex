\section{Task 4.2a}

From \autoref{fig:wp4_manifolds_system_wide}, manifolds of the Sun-Mars system do not intersect with Earth's orbit. 
However, the invariant manifolds intersect with the parking orbit around Mars (see \autoref{fig:wp4_manifolds_closeup}).
For this reason, the only feasible transfer trajectory using the invariant manifolds is a transfer from Mars L2 to the parking orbit.
